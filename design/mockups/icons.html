Custom premium icons — 3 directions

Eight of the app's icons drawn three ways, shown large and in the sidebar (English and Arabic). The first row is today's library icons (Phosphor) for comparison. These are quick sketches of the style, not final artwork.

Today · library icons (Phosphor)

A · Layered gradient

Each icon is built from two layers: a soft teal shape with a gentle gradient behind, a crisp deep-teal outline in front, and a glossy sunflower detail that catches the light. Depth without being heavy — premium, still calm for a work tool.

B · Glossy tiles

Like premium app icons: a teal tile with a light-to-deep gradient, a glass highlight across the top, an inner shadow and a soft drop shadow; the glyph is white with a glowing sunflower accent. The most "premium product" feel, the strongest brand presence.

C · Soft 3D

Shapes filled with a rounded teal gradient (light top, deep bottom), a white rim highlight and a soft coloured shadow, with a warm sunflower gradient accent — a tactile, clay-like look. Friendly and memorable; the most distinctive.
