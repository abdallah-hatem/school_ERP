Custom icons — round 3: simple and premium

Eight of the app's icons drawn three ways, shown large and in the sidebar (English and Arabic). The first row is today's library icons (Phosphor) for comparison. These are quick sketches of the style, not final artwork.

Today · library icons (Phosphor)

G · Precise monoline

One deep-teal line, 1.5px, rounded ends, drawn on a shared grid so every icon has the same weight and corner radius. The only extra is a small sunflower dot on the selected item. Quiet, exact, expensive-looking — the approach of Apple and Stripe.

H · Tinted duotone

The same precise line, plus one shape inside each icon filled with a faint tint of the same teal (about 12%). No gradients or shadows; just a hint of depth that makes them feel crafted rather than off-the-shelf.

I · Outline → solid when selected

Outline icons everywhere, and the selected sidebar item switches to a solid version of the same icon (like iOS). Simple at rest, a clear premium touch on the active item.
