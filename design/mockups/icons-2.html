Custom icons — round 2, three new directions

Eight of the app's icons drawn three ways, shown large and in the sidebar (English and Arabic). The first row is today's library icons (Phosphor) for comparison. These are quick sketches of the style, not final artwork.

Today · library icons (Phosphor)

D · Gradient line

One continuous rounded line that flows from teal into sunflower, with a soft glow — no fills. Modern and premium, like the icons in Linear or Arc; light on the page, so it suits a sidebar people stare at all day.

E · Blob duotone

A thin deep-teal glyph sitting on an organic pastel blob (sunflower, mint or sky, varied per area). Warm and human, close to premium illustration sets; the blobs give each module its own colour.

F · Flat colour illustrations

Tiny flat illustrations in the palette plus two supporting colours (coral, sky): each object is drawn as a small scene — a banknote with a coin, a receipt with a stamp. Most character and most "made for us"; best for a school product.
