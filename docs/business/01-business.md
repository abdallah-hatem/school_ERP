# §1 The business

- **DECIDED** — A school ERP built first for one private school in Egypt (a client job), later
  resold to other schools with per-school settings. Each school gets its own deployment and data.
- **DECIDED** — The school pays once for the system plus a monthly fee for hosting, support and
  maintenance. The school receives a licence; the code stays ours.
- **DECIDED** — The first module is **HR**: employee records, penalties, lateness and payroll. Other
  modules (students, academics, fees, exams…) come later.
- **DECIDED** — The school runs KG, primary and preparatory stages. Staff include teachers,
  administrators, supervisors, drivers, security, maintenance and nursing.
- **DECIDED** — The school's own rules (working hours, penalties, deductions) are **settings**, not
  code, so each school can match its own regulations (§7).
- **DECIDED** — Arabic and English everywhere, with right-to-left layout in Arabic.
