# 2026-11-21 — Every time of day reads 12-hour, with AM/PM

Every time the ERP shows is now 12-hour with the period: `4:07 PM` in English, `4:07 م` / `9:30 ص`
in Arabic — never `16:07`. Midnight is `12:00 AM`, noon `12:00 PM`. Digits stay Latin in both
languages, as for every figure. Times are on the school's clock (Cairo), whatever a device is set to.

This covers the Activity and History timelines (and their tooltips), "Last sign-in" on the Users
list, a user's page and My account, the sign-in lock ("locked until 2:15 PM"), the notifications
bell and screen ("Today, 7:00 AM"), the payroll rules note on an approved month, and the change
history of the work hours ("Work start: 7:30 AM → 8:00 AM"). The work start / end lists in
Settings → School show `7:30 AM`, `3:00 PM` (§7). Nothing stored changes: the work hours are still
saved as `07:30` / `15:00`, and the rules about them (half-hour steps, end after start) are the
same. The exports (bank sheet, payroll taxes sheet) carry no times. Decided by the owner.
