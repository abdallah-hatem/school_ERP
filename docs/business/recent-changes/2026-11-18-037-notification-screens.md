# 2026-11-18 — Notifications: the bell and the two preference screens

The notification screens (§11, option A, owner) are built, in the app only — no other channel is
mentioned anywhere. A **bell** beside the language switch shows how many notifications are unread
("99+" past 99) and refreshes every minute and whenever the person comes back to the tab. It opens
a short list over the page (on a phone, the whole screen): All or Unread, "Mark all as read", and a
link to the person's notification settings. Opening a notification marks it read and opens its
record; a group ("12 contracts end within 30 days") opens into its items first. Done ones stay in
the list, greyed, with who did them. **My account → Notifications** has one switch per kind the
person can receive; approvals are on and can't be changed ("Required"); a switch that differs from
the school's default says so and can be reset to it. **Settings → Notifications** (§7) has the same
list for the school's defaults, the **reminder timing** (an HR rule, changed by whoever may change
HR rules) and how long notifications are kept: the System admin changes the defaults and the
keeping; the HR manager sees them read-only.
