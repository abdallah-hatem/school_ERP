# 2026-11-18 — HR reminders

HR's **reminders** (§11) are now checked every morning at 07:00 school time (Cairo): a contract
ending in 30 days and again in 7 (the first is marked done when the second is sent), a probation end
in 7 days (the employee's own date, §3), the month's lateness not entered from the 25th, and the
month's payroll not prepared, or still a draft, from the 28th. Each goes to whoever may do the thing —
contracts and probation to whoever may edit that employee (their stage or team, or the whole school,
§2); lateness and payroll to whoever does them for the whole school — and is never sent twice, even
if the server was down that day (the next morning sends it once). Many contracts on one day make one
notification ("38 contracts end within 30 days"). A reminder goes away as soon as the thing is done:
the date changed, the employee deactivated, the month's lateness saved (even all zeros), a run
created, the run approved. A contract that already ended isn't chased.

The timings are new **HR rules** (§7), recorded in the settings history: contract reminder 30 days
(1–120) and again 7 (0 = none, fewer than the first), probation reminder 7 days (1–60), lateness and
payroll check days 25 and 28 (1–31; a day past the month's end is its last day). The days are GUESS
until the school confirms them. The bell that shows reminders comes with the notification screens.
