# 2026-11-24 — Contracts: the signed copy of each contract

The owner, testing locally: "right now I can't upload a photo of the contract so I can see it for
each employee".

- **Signed copy (§3 → Contracts):** each contract holds its signed copy as one or more files — PDF,
  JPG, PNG or WebP, each up to 10 MB, checked by their content — in upload order, because a phone
  photo is one page each. Any contract may get one (past, current or upcoming), not a cancelled one.
  A renewal's new contract starts with none; the old one keeps its own.
- **Where:** the employee's Contracts tab — the Documents column reads "Signed copy (2)" or "No
  signed copy", the row's ⋯ has "Upload signed copy" (on old contracts too) — the renewal screen's
  ⋯, and optionally "Add employee" (attached to the first contract after saving), Renew (one
  contract) and New contract. Bulk renew has no upload. A phone offers the camera, the photos or a
  PDF.
- **The viewer:** the pages in order; a photo inline and zoomable, a PDF opened in a new tab;
  Download, Remove (with a reason) and "Add pages".
- **Who (§2):** uploading and removing need Manage contracts, never on one's own contract; viewing
  needs View contracts covering that employee (a stage head: their own stage).
- **Kept (§9):** removing a page needs a reason and keeps the file, with who and when. Every page
  added or removed is in the employee's History, and each page opened is recorded, like the letters.
