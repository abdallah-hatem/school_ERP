# 2026-11-18 — Penalties in the audit log

Penalties in the audit log (§9, §4, T29, T31): recording, approving and cancelling a penalty are
now on **Activity**, each saved together with its change, naming the employee and the violation
with the date, the level, the penalty and its status (approving: "Pending → Approved"; cancelling:
the reason). A refused penalty leaves nothing. The employee's **History** tab lists their penalties
too, for whoever may see them. Activity's search now also finds an employee — and their penalties —
by **employee code**, as its placeholder said. Every action in the system is now recorded.
