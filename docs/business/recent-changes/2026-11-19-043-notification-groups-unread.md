# 2026-11-19 — Notification groups and the Unread tab

From the live check of the notifications (§11): a group's title now counts only what still waits
("2 penalties waiting for your approval" once one of three is approved; "3 penalties — all handled"
when none is left, with "Approved by …" when one person approved them all). The Unread tab shows
exactly what the bell counts — a done notification is under All only — and empties as soon as
"Mark all as read" is pressed. Everyone sees the school's name and logo in the app, not only people
who may view the settings.
