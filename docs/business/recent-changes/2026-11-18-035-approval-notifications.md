# 2026-11-18 — Approval notifications

HR now sends its two approval notifications (§11). **"Penalty waiting for your approval"** goes,
the moment a penalty is recorded, to everyone who may approve penalties for that employee — the
whole school, or their own stage or team (§2) — except the person who recorded it. A recording that
is refused tells no one. When one approver approves it or anyone cancels it, it shows as done for
everyone ("Approved by …", "Cancelled by …") and leaves their unread count; several recorded before
the approver looks make one notification ("3 penalties waiting for your approval").
**"Payroll ready to approve"** goes to whoever may approve payroll when a draft month is created or
recalculated and none of its penalties is pending; a penalty recorded in the month makes it done
(the next recalculation sends it again), and approving the month marks it approved. Its net shows
only to people who see the school's payroll totals. Neither can be turned off. Nothing is shown
yet: the bell comes with the screens.
