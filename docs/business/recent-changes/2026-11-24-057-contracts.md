# 2026-11-24 — Contracts: history, renewal season, notices, early termination

Approved by the owner as drafted (spec `docs/specs/2026-11-24-contracts.md`; its OPEN points take
their stated defaults).

- **Contract history (§3):** each employee has one contract per contract year, kept forever. The
  employee's contract start / end are the current contract; they are set when the employee is added
  and change afterwards only through Contracts — no longer on the employee form. Every employee who
  existed got one contract from their current dates.
- **Renewal season (§3):** HR → Contracts shows one contract year: every contract ending in it, the
  decision for each (renew, not renewing — the school's decision, the employee gave notice, early
  termination, ended), five tiles and bulk renew (all or nothing). Renewing creates next year's
  contract (1 June – 31 May; a mid-year contract keeps its own cycle). Nothing renews by itself; a
  contract past its end with no decision shows "Ended, no decision". Not renewed → "To deactivate"
  the day after the end; HR deactivates (never automatic).
- **Notices (§3):** HR records the employee's written notice (date received, scanned letter). Outside
  the window (first day of term 2 – 30 April) it is still recorded, marked Early / Late. A notice
  can be withdrawn while the contract runs.
- **Early termination (§3):** requested from the first day of term 1 to its last; approved → the
  contract ends on the last day of term 1; rejected → final, a new request is needed.
- **Ending a contract (§3):** Article 148, resignation by agreement, death or other — reason, date
  (today or later, within the contract), note, optional attachment. It can't cut into a month whose
  payroll is approved. The employee's future reminders go away.
- **Who (§2):** View contracts — HR manager, HR officer, principal (all), stage head (own stage);
  Manage contracts — HR manager. Nobody acts on their own contract, a System admin neither.
- **School calendar (§7):** per contract year in Settings → HR rules: first and last day of term 1,
  first day of term 2, the non-renewal deadline. Without it, notices and requests are marked
  "Calendar not set" and the renewal screen says so.
- **Reminders (§11):** renewal decisions due (from 1 March), non-renewal window opens (first day of
  term 2) and closes soon (7 days before 30 April), early termination waiting; both timings are
  settings. "Contract ends soon" now also goes away when a renewal decision is made.
- **Penalties (§4):** the repetition window (contract year, 1 June – 31 May) is unchanged.
