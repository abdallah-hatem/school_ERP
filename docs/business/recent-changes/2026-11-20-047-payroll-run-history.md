# 2026-11-20 — A payroll run has a History tab

A payroll run's page now has two tabs under its header: **Employees** (the lines, as before) and
**History** — who created the run, recalculated it (totals before → after), added or removed an
adjustment (the employee, type, amount and reason), approved it, and Accounting returning it to HR
or marking it transferred, newest first, with the same field and person filters as an employee's
History. Amounts read in pounds and statuses in words, in English and Arabic. It shows for whoever
may open the run and the activity log; it needs the whole school's payroll, as the run's entries on
Activity do. Switching tabs keeps the header. An employee's History keeps listing their penalties
(recorded, approved, cancelled); lateness months and payroll runs stay off it, because those entries
hold several employees' figures. The run's figures read as a person would write them, on History and on Activity: Created shows the
net and the number of employees, Approved shows Draft → Approved and the net, Recalculated only the
figures that changed ("No change in totals" if none did), and "Need review" is never listed. The log
still keeps every figure. Decided by the owner (2026-11-20).
