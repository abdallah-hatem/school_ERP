# 2026-11-19 — "Who is this person in HR?" instead of "Linked employee"

"Linked employee" on the user form didn't say what it was for. It now asks **"Who is this person in
HR?"** — pick their employee record — and the users list, the user's page and My account call it
**Employee record** ("No employee record" when there is none). The hint says that only roles
limited to their stage, their team or themselves need it, and that without it they see nothing.
When such a role is picked and no employee record is, the form warns "This person will see no
records until you pick who they are in HR"; saving still works. The scope descriptions on the
Roles screen say "the user's employee record". An employee's profile now says **"Signs in as:
<name>"** (a link to the user's page for those who may open users) or **"No sign-in account"**.
The welcome screen for someone whose roles open no screen no longer tells them they have no role
(§2). Nothing changes in who sees what. Decided by the owner (2026-11-19).
