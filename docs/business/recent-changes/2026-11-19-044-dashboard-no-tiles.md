# 2026-11-19 — Home for a role that sees the dashboard but none of its figures

A role allowed to see the HR dashboard but not employees, payroll or penalties used to land on an
empty Home: just the greeting, because each figure on the dashboard also needs the permission of
the screen it comes from. Such a role now gets the welcome instead — their name and a link to each
screen they may open, then My account (§2). The figures rule itself is unchanged: no salary totals
or penalty counts without the screen's permission. The "View the HR dashboard" permission now says
so in the Roles screen. Found by the owner; the fix recommended by Claude, owner to confirm.
