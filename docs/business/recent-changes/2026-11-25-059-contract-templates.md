# 2026-11-25 — Contract templates and printed copies

Approved by the owner as drafted (spec `docs/specs/2026-11-24-contract-templates.md`, mockup option
A), with both open points on their defaults: one school signatory in settings, PDFs made on the
server.

- **Templates (§3, §7):** Settings → HR → Contract templates — several templates, each with English
  and Arabic names, an Arabic text and an optional English one, edited in a Word-style editor with a
  Fields panel (19 fields) and optional phrases `[ … ]`. One may be the default for each work system.
  Never deleted, only hidden; each text change is a new version, and copies keep the version they
  were printed from. The seed brings one generic sample, "Full-time contract".
- **Printing (§3):** "Print contract" on the employee's Contracts tab and the renewal list's ⋯ menu
  — template, language, the filled A4 preview with its warnings, Edit text for this copy only, then
  Print or Download PDF (the phone's share sheet). Salary in figures and in words. A template with a
  salary field needs access to that employee's salary. Never one's own contract, never a cancelled
  one. A change since the preview is refused and the preview reloads.
- **Printed copies (§3):** every copy is kept on its contract with its PDF (made on the server, the
  same file every time it opens), who printed it and when. A copy that holds a salary opens only for
  those who may see that salary.
- **Settings (§7):** the school's address and its signatory (name and title), in English and Arabic.
- **Who (§2):** Manage contract templates — HR manager; printing — Manage contracts; opening a copy
  — View contracts.
- **Audit (§9):** templates (create, edit, hide, show) and copies (printed, opened).
