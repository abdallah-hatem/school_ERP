# 2026-11-18 — Notifications: the mechanism

Notifications (§11, §2, §7): the mechanism behind the bell is built — nothing sends a notification
yet (HR's approvals and reminders come next) and the screens are still Planned. Who is told is
decided by what they may do and the employees they cover; the person who caused something isn't
told; many of one kind make one grouped notification while it is unread; the same thing is never
sent twice; when an approval is done by one person it shows as done for everyone; someone who loses
access stops seeing those notifications. Each person can turn a kind off for themselves (approvals
can't be turned off); the school sets the defaults. Two new permissions (§2, owner): **View
notification settings** (System admin, HR manager) and **Manage notification settings** (System
admin). Notifications are kept 90 days after reading, 30 days after an approval is done, at most
365 days (§7), and removed every night. Reading them isn't recorded in the activity log; changing
one's preferences or the school's notification settings is.
