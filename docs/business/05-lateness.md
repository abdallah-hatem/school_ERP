# §5 Lateness and early leave

- **DECIDED** — Work starts at 07:30 and ends at 15:00 (settings, §7).
- **DECIDED** — Morning lateness and early leave are **added up for the month** per employee, in
  minutes.
- **DECIDED (demo)** — HR enters each employee's total minutes for the month by hand. Importing them
  from the fingerprint device is Planned.
- **DECIDED** — The days deducted come from the **lateness rule**: the 1st hour of accumulated
  minutes costs **¼ day**, and the 2nd hour costs **½ day** more (¾ day for 61–120 minutes).
- **GUESS** — Any minutes inside an hour count as that hour: 1–60 minutes = ¼ day.
- **GUESS** — Each further hour beyond the 2nd costs another ½ day. The client's rules stop at the
  2nd hour; this needs confirming.
- **GUESS** — No grace minutes. A grace setting exists (default 0). Grace is subtracted from the
  month's total first; the hour steps apply to what is left.
- **DECIDED** — The rule is a setting: a list of hour steps with their day cost, plus the cost of
  each step beyond the list (§7).
- **DECIDED** — Lateness days reduce pay in that month's payroll (§6). Entries can't change once that
  month's payroll is approved.
- **DECIDED** — Minutes are whole numbers from 0 up to 6,000 a month.
